# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Product managers, founders, engineers, support teams, and their customers use Reflet while turning scattered feedback into decisions. Internal teams triage, prioritize, assign, and resolve requests; customers submit ideas and bugs, follow progress, and see when work ships.

## Product Purpose

Reflet collects product feedback close to where it happens, adds enough context to make it actionable, and connects each report to planning, implementation, and the customer-facing feedback loop. Success means fewer lost reports, faster reproduction and resolution, clear prioritization, and visible follow-through for the reporter.

## Brand Personality

Clear, collaborative, quietly confident. The product should feel approachable without becoming playful, technical without becoming intimidating, and polished without slowing down frequent operational work.

## Anti-references

Avoid dense enterprise ticketing software, noisy analytics dashboards, novelty-first developer tools, and generic AI-workflow styling. Reflet should not rely on decorative gradients, excessive cards, inflated radii, unexplained automation, or unfamiliar controls where a standard product pattern works better.

## Design Principles

- Capture context at the source so users describe less and teams infer less.
- Keep the main action obvious, then disclose technical detail only when it helps resolution.
- Make every state recoverable; prefer inline feedback, optimistic updates, and undoable actions.
- Preserve continuity from report to roadmap to shipped update instead of creating disconnected records.
- Earn trust through readable hierarchy, predictable controls, and explicit system status.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Every workflow must support keyboard and touch input, visible focus, sufficient contrast, semantic labels, reduced motion, and alternatives to color-only meaning. Feedback capture must remain usable when screenshots, DOM inspection, browser APIs, or optional metadata are unavailable.
